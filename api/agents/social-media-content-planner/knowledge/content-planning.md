# Content planning

Pick three or four content pillars, such as behind the scenes, customer
stories, tips and offers, and rotate through them.

Keep promotion to about one post in five. The rest should be useful or
entertaining, so people keep following.

Customers' most common questions are the best source of post ideas: answer
one question per post.

A steady schedule beats bursts. Three posts a week, every week, works better
than ten posts one week and none the next.
