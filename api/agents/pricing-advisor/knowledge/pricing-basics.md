# Pricing basics

Your floor is the full cost of one item, including ingredients, packaging,
labour and a share of rent. Never price below it for long.

Customers compare against something. Showing a premium option next to your
main one makes the main one look like good value.

Bundles raise the average sale: pair a best seller with a slower item at a
small discount.

Competing only on price is a race to the bottom. Compete on quality,
convenience or service, and price to match.
